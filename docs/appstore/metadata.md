## Version 1.1.0

### What's New

Optional focus tabs for Running, Jogging, Walk, Steps and Energy, each with daily, weekly and monthly views and their own goals. Turn them on in You → Tabs (only Running is on by default).

Summary now shows a ring for every tab you enable. Sessions stay on their own tab.

Checks the App Store about once a day for updates and can re-prompt for any Apple Health types you have not answered yet. If Health access is on but no data shows, a banner points you to Health → Apps → RunCheck.

Works offline. Your Health data still never leaves your iPhone.

### Promotional text

Your walks, jogs and runs from Apple Health, with Summary plus optional focus tabs, daily goals, and private on-device stats. No account, no ads.

### Description

RunCheck turns the steps and workouts already in Apple Health into clear walk, jog and run stats. No account, no ads: your data stays on your iPhone. Works offline.

SUMMARY, DAY TO MONTH
• Today, Week and Month totals with rings against your goals
• Walk, jog and run tiles for time, pace, energy, steps and sessions
• Sessions list with speed, pace, tempo and a speed graph

FOCUS TABS YOU CONTROL
• Optional Running, Jogging, Walk, Steps and Energy tabs
• Each tab has Daily, Week and Month views and its own goals
• Only Running is on by default; turn others on in You → Tabs

MAKE IT YOURS
• Daily, weekly and monthly goals for the tabs you use
• Kilometres or miles, and your own jog and run speed thresholds
• Minimum session length and how short breaks are joined

WORKS WITH WHAT YOU ALREADY HAVE
RunCheck uses the steps your iPhone counts, workouts from Apple Watch, and walks and runs that other apps save to Apple Health.

PRIVATE BY DESIGN
RunCheck only reads Apple Health and never writes to it. Your health data never leaves your iPhone, isn't stored in iCloud and is never used for advertising. About once a day it asks Apple's App Store lookup for its latest version number (app ID and country only) so it can offer an update.

RunCheck shows activity stats only. It isn't a medical device and doesn't give medical advice.
