### Description

RunCheck turns the steps and workouts already in Apple Health into clear walk, jog and run stats. No account, no internet, no ads: your data stays on your iPhone.

SEE YOUR DAY, WEEK AND MONTH
• Today: steps against your daily goal, distance, energy and active time
• Week: Sunday to Saturday, swipe between weeks and tap any day
• Month: monthly totals with a tile for each week

WALK, JOG OR RUN, SORTED FOR YOU
• Movement is sorted by speed into walks, jogs and runs
• Real sessions are listed, not pacing around the house or office
• Open a session to see its speed, pace, tempo and a speed graph

MAKE IT YOURS
• Daily step goal, kilometres or miles
• Your own jog and run speed thresholds
• Minimum session length and how short breaks are joined

WORKS WITH WHAT YOU ALREADY HAVE
RunCheck uses the steps your iPhone counts, workouts from Apple Watch, and walks and runs that other apps save to Apple Health.

PRIVATE BY DESIGN
RunCheck only reads Apple Health and never writes to it. Your health data never leaves your iPhone, isn't stored in iCloud and is never used for advertising.

RunCheck shows activity stats only. It isn't a medical device and doesn't give medical advice.
