# RunCheck Support

RunCheck shows your daily steps and your walk, jog, and run stats on iPhone, using data from Apple Health.

## Common questions

**My steps or totals show zero.**
Open the Health app, tap your profile picture, then **Apps → RunCheck**, and turn on the data types. You can also check permissions in RunCheck's **You** tab.

**Where does the data come from?**
From Apple Health: your iPhone's step counting, an Apple Watch, or any app that saves walks and runs to Health. RunCheck only reads it.

**How is a session counted as a walk, jog, or run?**
By your average speed. You can change the jog and run speed thresholds in the **You** tab.

**Does RunCheck need an account or internet?**
No. Everything stays on your iPhone. See the [Privacy Policy](privacy).
